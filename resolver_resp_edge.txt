## 🛠️ 3 MANDATORY REMEDIATION STEPS
*Non-negotiable. Must be implemented before any production pilot or regulatory assertion.*

### 1. Enforce Durable Intent via Append-Only WAL (Fix Async State & Crash Recovery)
- **Action:** Before returning the receipt to the caller, execute a synchronous WAL append of a `PENDING_PQ_FINALIZATION` record containing: `eventId`, hot-path receipt hash, canonical payload hash, and `enclave_epoch`.
- **Protocol Rule:** The hot path **must not return** until `fsync` confirms the durability of the pending event.
- **Recovery:** On enclave restart, scan WAL for `PENDING_PQ_FINALIZATION` without a `PQ_SIGNED` tail record. Idempotently replay missing signatures and emit operational alerts. This eliminates the "black hole for auditors" and converts fire-and-forget into deterministic deferred completion.

### 2. Canonicalize Payload & Bind PQ to Hot-Path Receipt (Fix Quantum Gap & ID Collision)
- **Action:** Replace `Date.now()` receipt IDs with deterministic `H(tenantId || scopedNonce || policyHash || paramsHash || seqCounter)`.
- **Binding Requirement:** The `signMLDSA()` payload **must** sign a canonical commitment root:
  `CommitmentRoot = H(HotPathSignature || AllowDenyDecision || CanonicalParamsHash || PolicyID || EnclaveAttestationQuote)`
- **Protocol Rule:** The PQ signature no longer asserts "a log exists at time T". It cryptographically attests that **this specific hot-path authorization was finalized by this specific enclave lineage**. Any drift between hot and audit paths becomes mathematically provable and instantly invalidates the receipt.

### 3. Isolate ML-DSA Execution & Enforce Backpressure (Fix Resource Starvation & Silent Failures)
- **Action:** Move `signMLDSA` out of the main event loop into a bounded `worker_threads` pool with a strict concurrency limit (e.g., `maxQueue=50`).
- **Backpressure Policy:** If the queue is full, the gateway must either queue with explicit exponential backoff or return `503: PQ_AUDIT_BACKPRESSURE` with the Ed25519 receipt explicitly marked `pq_status: PENDING_DEGRADED`. Never silently drop.
- **Code Correction:** Fix the missing `await` on `signMLDSA`. Replace `catch(console.error)` with durable error routing: log to WAL as `PQ_SIGNED: FAILED`, trigger compliance escalation, and block tenant spend limits until resolution. This prevents the kill switch from becoming a self-DDoS vector and eliminates side-channel timing leakage in the hot loop.
